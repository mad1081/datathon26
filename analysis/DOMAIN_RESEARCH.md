# Domain research: cash operations, Kazakhstan

Checked: 2026-10-17. Purpose: an actionable public-source map for the ForteBank Datathon. This is an engineering research note, not a legal conclusion, a confirmed dataset taxonomy, or a claim that any particular video contains these events. No dataset images or scene descriptions were sent to web tools.

## 1. Primary source catalogue

### R231 — cash operations and collection

[National Bank Board resolution No. 231, 29 November 2019, registration No. 19680](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680). [Official legacy text](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680), [information](https://www.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680/info), [history](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680/history).

Scope includes Kazakhstan second-tier banks and cash-collection organizations. The official record is marked updated, rather than repealed. Its information page reports 12 July 2026 as the modification date; history search also exposes entries dated 30 June 2026. Indexed consolidated text includes the 2025 and 2026 amendments. Do not use a third-party page's March 2026 date as proof of the latest consolidated version.

Verified clause map, paraphrased:

| Clause | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| 9 | Bank provides equipment for checking banknote authenticity and fitness. |
| 19(1) | Customer identity check may use identity documents or the digital-document service. |
| 19(2) | Incoming notes are counted individually; coins individually; authenticity and fitness checked. |
| 19(6) | Customer receives a document confirming cash acceptance. |
| 20 | Acceptance/counting occur with the customer present; earlier receipts are stored in lockable cash-storage devices. |
| 21 | Multiple incoming cash documents are handled separately. |
| 22 | Cashier may not leave the workplace before completing customer service. |
| 80 | Internal procedures for keys, stamps and valuables must prevent unauthorized use. |
| 103 | Collection team is supplied protective equipment, including body armour and helmets, and service weapons. |
| 107 | Collector checks bag integrity, sealing and accompanying records. |
| 108 | Team leader checks the bag when receiving it from the collecting worker. |

### A71 — changes that matter for phones and personal belongings

[National Bank resolution No. 71, 10 November 2025, registration No. 37429](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2500037429); [official legacy text](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2500037429).

This amendment replaces R231 paragraph 12: employees' personal belongings, money and valuables are stored outside the cash unit. It expressly allows medicines, medical devices and communication devices without photo/video recording functions. It also changes paragraphs 19(2), 19(3) and 31(2). The change is visible in the consolidated R231 text.

This supplies a specific basis for investigating an employee's personal camera phone in the cash unit. It does not establish that every rectangular object is a phone, every phone belongs to the employee, or a customer's phone is prohibited. A personal object should first be identified with sufficient visual confidence.

### A19 — cash withdrawal sequence

[National Bank resolution No. 19, 28 February 2022, registration No. 27073](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2200027073); [official legacy text](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2200027073).

The amendment supplies R231 paragraph 31: identify the customer (including through digital documents), check signatures, prepare the amount, count it in the customer's presence, issue money, sign/stamp the expense documents and give the customer a confirming document. The signature-check subparagraph was subsequently amended in 2025; do not present this amendment alone as the complete current paragraph.

For this project, paragraph 31(4) is a useful reference for a clearly visible withdrawal without counting. Electronic workflows and cropped footage make missing-paperwork claims much weaker.

### R14 — premises and physical security

[National Bank resolution No. 14, 24 February 2020, registration No. 20075](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2000020075); [official legacy text](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2000020075), [history](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2000020075/history).

The official record is updated; the latest history entry retrieved is 24 December 2024. Paragraph 8 requires separation of the cash unit by a closable service entrance, with a possible additional loading entrance. Paragraph 7 permits the bank to determine whether a separate recounting room is needed.

[Amending resolution No. 88, 24 December 2024, registration No. 35552](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2400035552) establishes transitional wording through 1 January 2027. Under the transitional paragraph 16, each cash desk has a separate entrance with a lockable door; protective arrangements cover the walls/front, and cash transfer uses special transport devices operated from inside. Do not accidentally apply the future 2027 technical wording to the hackathon date.

These are premises requirements. A door being momentarily open is not, by itself, proof that the premises lack a lockable door. Video cannot establish construction thickness, certification, alarm functionality or unseen security coverage.

## 2. Proposed observation checklist for local review

These are our engineering hypotheses, not labels inferred from the dataset. Rank them after the actual videos are reviewed. An event should enter the supported taxonomy only if we can define both a positive example and normal counterexamples.

| Candidate | Source to inspect | Evidence our detector would need | Major false-positive route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cashier leaves during an unfinished operation | R231 §22 | Track customer, cashier and transaction state across a continuous interval. | Operation finished; cashier only reaches within work area; camera cuts. |
| Earlier customer's cash remains exposed during a new operation | R231 §20 | Follow the same bundle through one completed transaction into another. | Bundle belongs to current customer; money is being returned or counted; identity of bundle lost. |
| Incoming cash accepted without an observable count | R231 §19(2), §20 | Full handover-to-storage sequence; manually counted and machine-counted paths both checked. | Counting occurs outside view or before clip; apparatus operation is occluded. |
| Withdrawal given without an observable count | A19 → R231 §31(4) | Full prepare-to-handover interval; verify applicable issue mode. | Sealed-bundle or other permitted workflow; preparation not recorded. |
| Personal camera phone or belongings in employee cash area | A71 → R231 §12 | Reliable object identification plus employee-side location and use. | Customer shows digital identity; work terminal; permitted camera-free communication device; medicine. |
| Collection bag handled without visible inspection | R231 §107–108 | Identify the collection stage, bag and full recipient interaction. | Brief inspection missed by sampling; handover stage misidentified; bag/label too small. |
| Protective equipment apparently missing on collection team | R231 §103 | Body and head visible through the relevant collection stage. | Armour concealed by clothing; off-camera team member; rule is equipment provision, not an unlimited all-moments visual mandate. |
| Cash unit access appears unsecured | R14 §8, transitional §16 | Understand room boundaries and actual access path. | A door is briefly open during authorized access; layout certification cannot be inferred. |
| Key/stamp exposed to another person | R231 §80 | Identify key/stamp and actual access/use, not mere proximity. | Authorized employee; legitimate handover under internal procedures unavailable to us. |

Do not infer theft, intent, unauthorized status, or named identity from appearance. Cash moved towards a pocket is an observable movement hypothesis; proving ownership, concealment and illegal appropriation requires more than a generic action caption. Do not force it into a legal class until the applicable public rule and visible evidence are established.

## 3. Rejected shortcuts and scope boundaries

- A model that says "looks suspicious" supplies neither a regulation nor a verified event. Store observed actions separately from rule evaluation.
- No paper identity card does not prove no identity check: R231 explicitly includes digital identity. The case also forbids facial identification; do not implement face matching to audit this duty.
- No visible UV lamp does not prove no authenticity check: combined counters/detectors and occlusion are possible. Do not claim banknote authenticity from the supplied camera resolution.
- A clip that begins or ends mid-operation cannot establish every missing step. Missing-action events need an interval long enough to include the required step.
- The customer area and the employee cash unit are different zones. A universal phone/bag detector would confuse normal customer possessions with employee restrictions.
- A second person is not automatically an unauthorized person. Supervisors, controllers and collectors can legitimately attend.
- Do not use arbitrary fixed coordinates as the definition of a protected zone; the final camera angle changes.
- Books, websites or older bank manuals may repeat obsolete instructions. Match every citation to its issuer, scope and effective version.

## 4. Explicitly unsuitable sources

[No. 58 of 3 March 2001, V010001482_](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V010001482_) is repealed by No. 231, effective 1 January 2020. Its very detailed old instructions about keys, personal possessions and cashier routines rank highly in search; they are not current mandatory authority.

[No. 250 of 24 August 2012, V1200008080](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1200008080) is replaced by the 2020 premises rules. National Bank internal premises/access rules and public-sector treasury cash rules have a different institutional scope from a second-tier commercial bank. Russian, Uzbek or Belarusian cash rules may inform background research, but the case requires Kazakhstan sources for the actual normative rationale.

## 5. Implementation decision for the remaining hackathon

Our proposed rule catalogue should be small and versioned: `rule_id`, issuer/document/date, paragraph, URL, short paraphrase, scope, necessary visible evidence, permitted alternatives, unobservable conditions, and review status. Runtime inference chooses only from reviewed catalogue entries. The model must not invent legal citations.

Review the legal map and the supplied footage in parallel. A quick first pass can test whether candidate actions are even visible; a second pass can enrich binary labels with event class, interval, evidence timestamp and uncertainty. Human review of roughly 22 minutes of unique footage is likely a better use of early time than building a broad legal retrieval system.

For an omission, record an evidence interval internally and a consistent output timestamp, such as the handover/storage boundary after which the required visible step is absent from the observed sequence. That timestamp policy is our design choice, not a statutory instruction.

Keep reported limitations concrete: missing transaction beginning/end, hand/object occlusion, small seals or notes, uncertain role/authorization, hidden protective gear, unknown electronic workflow, and changed camera geometry. These limitations should come from actual validation once available.
