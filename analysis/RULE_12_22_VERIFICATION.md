# Narrow verification: R231 paragraphs 12 and 22

Checked: 2026-10-17. This is a provenance note for two clauses already retrieved from indexed official Adilet pages. It is not a downloaded authenticated copy of the complete regulation. Direct page/PDF downloads timed out or returned a JavaScript-only shell.

## Paragraph 12: personal belongings

Primary consolidated page: https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680

Primary amending act: https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2500037429

Primary cross-reference record: https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2500037429/links

Short exact excerpt from the amending act (R231 paragraph 12 replacement):

> Хранение личных вещей, наличных денег и ценностей работников банка осуществляется вне кассового узла в порядке, установленном внутренними документами банка.

The following sentence permits medicines, medical devices and communication devices without photo/video recording functions. This is a paraphrase, not an additional quotation.

The indexed consolidated page includes the note that paragraph 12 is in the edition of National Bank resolution No. 71 of 10 November 2025. Its implementation condition is ten calendar days after the first official publication. The amendment's registration number is 37429, registration date 18 November 2025.

Successful original search queries, with `domains: ["adilet.zan.kz"]` where stated:

- `"кассовых" "12." "фото" "2025"` (domain filter): returned amendment and cross-reference text, including full replacement paragraph.
- `"кассовый работник" "наличными деньгами" "11." "2019"` (domain filter), batched with `"кассовых" "11." "12." "13." "231"`: returned the consolidated official page including paragraph 12 and its exception.
- Followup repeated the first query: primary `/links` record again confirmed the 2025 amendment and camera-function wording. Repeating the second batch returned the consolidated page's amendment note, but less surrounding text.

Application limit: the clause concerns employees' personal items in the cash unit. A customer's digital-identity phone, a work terminal, permitted camera-free communications equipment or medication is not automatically a matching event. Object presence must not be labelled as misconduct without role, location and object evidence.

## Paragraph 22: leaving during customer service

Primary consolidated page: https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680

Canonical identifier: https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680

Short exact excerpt from the consolidated regulation:

> Кассовому работнику не допускается покидать рабочее место до окончания обслуживания клиента.

Original successful batch used `domains: ["adilet.zan.kz"]` with these queries:

- `Кассовому работнику не допускается покидать рабочее место до окончания обслуживания клиента`
- `кассовых операций 231 2019 наличные личные`
- `кассовых операций 231 2019 металлический шкаф ящики стола`

The result for the legacy consolidated page reproduced paragraphs 17–24, including numbered paragraphs 19, 20, 21 and 22, and subsequent text. A later exact-phrase search also returned the official bilingual comparison page https://old.adilet.zan.kz/kaz/docs/V1900019680/compare/rus with the corresponding Kazakh rule. Direct opening of the legacy page still timed out during the followup.

Application limit: this supports evaluating a departure before service finishes. It does not make every momentary stand-up, reach, out-of-frame movement or departure after a completed transaction a violation.
